Our story

Three generations of Thai rice makers

สามรุ่นของคนทำข้าวไทย

Pat, the third generation of Rossukon Fermented Rice, grew up eating khao taen, the traditional Thai rice cracker. She loved the taste but not the big, messy sheets that went stale so quickly.

So she created Paddii: a bite-sized Thai rice snack that stays crispy, keeps your hands clean, and is made for everyone, every day.
